Support

Email rohanjshworks@gmail.com and include your macOS version and what you were doing. Please do not send client information, transcripts or notes. SessionVault never sends them to the developer, and support does not need them.

Requirements

A Mac with Apple silicon, macOS 26 or later, and Apple Intelligence turned on with its model downloaded, in a supported language and region. Without it, SessionVault can still store transcripts and give you a note template to fill in, but cannot draft notes.

Common questions

"Draft note" shows a template instead of a draft

The on-device model is unavailable or declined the text. Check System Settings → Apple Intelligence, wait for its model to finish downloading, and try again. If the model declines a session, it gives no reason; complete the template yourself. The template includes the risk-related statements quoted from the transcript.

Is the note final?

No. Every note is an AI-written draft and may be wrong or incomplete. Read it against the transcript, edit it, then mark it as reviewed. Risk-related statements are quoted word for word from the transcript so you can check them; they are not an assessment.

Back up, restore, and moving to a new Mac

Your encryption key never leaves your Mac, so a backup is the only way to recover or move your records. Use Backup in the sidebar to create an encrypted file and keep the passphrase somewhere safe. We cannot recover either.

Microphone or speech permission

Allow SessionVault in System Settings → Privacy & Security → Microphone and Speech Recognition.
