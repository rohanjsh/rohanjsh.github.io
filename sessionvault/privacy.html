Privacy Policy

SessionVault for Therapists · Last updated: 25 October 2026

SessionVault is designed so that your clinical information stays on your Mac. This policy says what the app does and does not do with your data.

What we collect

Nothing. The developer does not receive, collect, store or have access to any of your data. SessionVault has no accounts, no servers, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs.

What stays on your Mac

  • Session transcripts, draft notes and your process notes are saved in an encrypted vault on your Mac. The encryption key is kept in your Mac's Keychain and does not leave the device.
  • Audio you record or import is turned into text on your Mac and is deleted right after a successful transcription. Any leftovers are removed the next time the app starts.
  • Drafting notes uses the on-device language model built into macOS (Apple Intelligence). The transcript is processed on your Mac. SessionVault has no network access and cannot send it anywhere.
  • Process notes are stored separately and are never given to the language model.

Network

The app is built without network permission. It cannot connect to the internet. Apple's system services may download the on-device speech or language model to your Mac as part of macOS, which is governed by Apple's privacy policy, not by SessionVault.

Backups

You can export an encrypted backup file protected by a passphrase you choose. You decide where to save it. We cannot recover your data or your passphrase. If you lose both your Mac's key and your backups, your records cannot be recovered.

Copying notes

When you press Copy, the note is placed on your Mac's clipboard, where other apps and clipboard managers can read it. SessionVault clears it after 60 seconds if nothing else was copied.

Your responsibilities

SessionVault is a drafting aid. Notes it writes are drafts that can be wrong or incomplete, and you must review and edit every one before relying on it. Record sessions only with your client's informed consent. You are responsible for meeting any legal, ethical and record-keeping duties that apply to your practice. SessionVault keeps data on your Mac, but it does not by itself make your practice compliant with any law or regulation.

Children

SessionVault is a professional tool for therapists and is not directed at children.
