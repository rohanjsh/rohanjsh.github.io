Privacy Policy

Hush · Last updated: 28 October 2026

The short version

The developer collects nothing. Hush keeps everything on your iPhone. There is no account, no server and no tracking. We do not collect, receive or sell any data about you.

What Hush stores

The dates of your periods and what you choose to log: flow, symptoms, mood, notes and optional test results. It is saved in a private database on your iPhone, shared only with Hush's own widgets.

Apple Health

If you allow it, Hush reads period history and Apple Watch measurements (resting heart rate, heart rate variability, wrist temperature, sleep) to give you a head start and a body baseline. Hush only reads. It never writes to Health, and what it reads stays on your iPhone.

Logging by voice

Only on iPhones with Apple Intelligence. When you tap the microphone, your words are turned into text on your iPhone and read by Apple's on-device model. Nothing is sent to any server and no audio is saved. The text itself is not kept either, only the log it produces. iOS shows its own standard speech permission prompt, whose wording is Apple's; Hush requests on-device recognition only.

What leaves your iPhone

Nothing, unless you choose to share it: a backup file or a visit brief PDF goes where you send it. Opening a link or an email goes through Safari or Mail, not through Hush. Purchases are handled by Apple.

Backups by Apple

If you use iCloud or computer backups of your iPhone, Apple may include Hush's data in them, under your Apple account's own protections. Hush cannot see those backups.

Deleting your data

In Hush, Settings > Erase everything removes all logs, periods and settings from this iPhone. Deleting the app does the same.

Children

Hush collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Not medical advice

Hush shows patterns and estimates from your own logs. It does not diagnose anything and must not be used as birth control.

Changes and contact
