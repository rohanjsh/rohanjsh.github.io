Support

Email rohanjshworks@gmail.com with your iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing. Please don't send screenshots or details of your private logs. Hush never sends them to the developer, and support doesn't need them.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Voice logging needs Apple Intelligence.

Common questions

Why don't I see an ovulation estimate?

Hush shows one only when your cycles are regular enough, after at least three finished cycles. Until then it says so instead of guessing. Optional LH test results, cervical fluid and Apple Watch temperature can sharpen it.

Why was my period start not accepted?

Hush won't start a new period fewer than 15 days after the last one, or on a future day. Spotting is saved but doesn't start a period. If the start was earlier than you logged, you can move it back up to 7 days.

How do I move my data to a new iPhone?

Settings > Data and support > Export a backup, then restore it in Hush on the new iPhone. Normal iPhone backups include Hush's data too.

How do I delete everything?

Settings > Erase everything, or delete the app.

Can I rely on Hush for birth control?

No. Hush gives estimates from your own logs and must not be used as birth control or for diagnosis.
