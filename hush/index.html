Hush

A calm, private cycle companion. Everything stays on your iPhone: no account, no server, no tracking.

What it does

  • Predictions as windows. Your next period and ovulation are shown as ranges with a confidence level and the number of cycles they are based on, never a single date.
  • Quick, forgiving logging. Log from Today, the Calendar, widgets, Control Center, Siri or by voice. Hush suggests what is usual for you and lets you correct it. There are no streaks and no guilt for gaps.
  • Private by design. No account, no analytics, no third-party SDKs. Your data never leaves your iPhone unless you export it.
  • Optional extras. Apple Health read-only import, app lock with Face ID, backup and visit brief PDF.

Requirements

iPhone with iOS 18 or later. Voice logging needs an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

Hush shows patterns and estimates from your own logs. It does not diagnose anything and must not be used as birth control.
