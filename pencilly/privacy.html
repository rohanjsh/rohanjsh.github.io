Privacy Policy

Pencilly · Last updated: 26 October 2026

Pencilly is built so that your calendar and your to-do list stay on your iPhone. This page says exactly what the app does, and does not do, with your information.

The short version

The developer collects nothing. Pencilly has no accounts, no servers of its own, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, no crash reporting and no third-party SDKs. We never receive, see or store your calendar, your tasks, your voice or your images.

What Pencilly does on your iPhone

  • Calendar. With your permission, Pencilly reads events from the calendars on your iPhone (including iCloud, Google or Outlook accounts you have added in iOS Settings) to find free time. It adds the plan you approve to a calendar called "Pencilly". That calendar is created in an account already on your phone, so, like any other calendar, your account provider (for example Apple or Google) may sync it with your other devices. That sync is between you and your provider; we are not involved.
  • What you type, paste or share. Text is turned into tasks on your iPhone. The "Plan in Pencilly" share option hands text or a screenshot from another app to Pencilly through storage that only Pencilly and its share extension can use, on your phone.
  • Screenshots and photos. When you scan one, the text in it is read on your iPhone with Apple's on-device text recognition. Pencilly can only see the pictures you pick; it does not browse your photo library, and it does not keep the image.
  • Voice. Speech is transcribed on your iPhone. If iOS cannot recognise your language on the device, voice input is turned off rather than sending audio to Apple. Audio is not recorded or saved.
  • The AI. Where Apple Intelligence is available, Pencilly uses the language model built into your iPhone to read your tasks. Otherwise it uses built-in rules. Both run on the device and neither sends your text anywhere.
  • Face ID (optional). If you turn on the app lock, Pencilly asks iOS to check your face or passcode. It never receives your biometric data.
  • Live Activities and the Dynamic Island. These show the title of your current task on the Lock Screen and in the Dynamic Island, so anyone who can see your Lock Screen can see it. You can turn Live Activities off for Pencilly in iOS Settings.

What is stored on your iPhone

  • Your settings: work hours, meals and other protected time, and breathing room between tasks.
  • The identifiers, titles and lengths of the tasks Pencilly added, so it can undo, re-plan and run Live Activities.
  • How many free plans you have used, and nothing else about your subscription: iOS tells the app whether Pro is active.

Deleting Pencilly removes this. Events it added to your calendar stay there until you delete them (the Undo button removes a plan; you can also delete the "Pencilly" calendar in your Calendar app).

Purchases

Pencilly Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple handles payment, and we never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. Apple may give developers aggregated sales reports that cannot identify you. Pencilly checks on the device whether your subscription is active.

Network

Pencilly itself does not send your information to anyone. The only connections involved are Apple's own services (the App Store for purchases, and your calendar provider's sync if you use one).

Children

Pencilly collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. Everything Pencilly knows is on your iPhone and under your control in the app, in Settings, and in your Calendar app. If you are in the EU, UK or California and have a question about your rights, email us below.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date above will change and the new version will be on this page. If a future version of Pencilly ever collected data, it would say so here first.
