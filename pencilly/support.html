Requirements

iPhone with iOS 26 or later, and calendar access. Apple Intelligence is optional: with it Pencilly understands tasks more flexibly; without it, built-in rules still plan your tasks.

Common questions

How do I undo a plan?

Right after tapping Add to calendar, tap Undo. Later, delete the events from the "Pencilly" calendar in your Calendar app, or delete the whole calendar.

Will it double-book me?

No. Pencilly reads every calendar on your phone and keeps a gap around existing events. It ignores events you declined, events marked "free", and all-day holidays and birthdays. All-day "out of office" events block the day. If your calendar changes while you're looking at a plan, Pencilly checks again when you tap Add and moves anything that now clashes.

It says "Using smart rules". Why?

Apple Intelligence isn't available or turned on for your iPhone, so Pencilly is using its built-in rules. Turn it on in Settings → Apple Intelligence & Siri where supported. Planning works either way.

Voice input says it can't recognise my language

To keep your voice on your iPhone, Pencilly only uses speech recognition that works on the device. If iOS doesn't support your language offline yet, type or scan instead.

The Dynamic Island doesn't show my task

It appears only while a planned task is under way. Check Settings → Pencilly → Live Activities, and that the task was added to the "Pencilly" calendar.

Free plans and Pencilly Pro

Your first 3 plans that you add to your calendar are free. After that, Pencilly Pro (yearly with a 7-day free trial, or monthly) lets you keep planning. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions. Use Restore Subscription on the paywall or in Pencilly's settings if you've reinstalled or changed phones. For a refund, request it from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.
