These are the plain-language terms for using Pencilly. They sit on top of Apple's standard license for apps, which is what actually governs your use of the app.

The license

Pencilly is licensed to you under Apple's standard Licensed Application End User License Agreement. Please read it. If anything on this page conflicts with it, Apple's agreement wins.

Free plans and Pencilly Pro

  • Free plans. Your first 10 plans that you add to your calendar are free. Pencilly counts them on your device.
  • Pencilly Pro. After that, planning needs Pencilly Pro, an auto-renewing subscription: $34.99 a year or $4.99 a month in the US. Prices vary by country and are shown in the app before you buy.
  • Free trial. The yearly plan includes a 7-day free trial for new subscribers. Unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the trial ends, your Apple ID is charged for the first year.
  • Renewal and cancellation. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation of purchase. The subscription renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.
  • Refunds and billing. Apple handles billing, so refunds are requested from Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com.

How Pencilly plans

Pencilly suggests times; you decide. Review each plan before you tap Add to calendar, and check anything important yourself. Plans are worked out from the text you give it and the events in your calendars, so a wrong or missing calendar event can lead to a clash. Pencilly is provided as is, without a promise that a plan will suit your day.

Your information

Pencilly keeps your calendar, tasks, voice and images on your iPhone. The privacy policy explains exactly what the app does and does not do with them.
